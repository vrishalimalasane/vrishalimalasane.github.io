About

A dashboard builder who started in QA

I'm a Data Analyst and BI Developer based in Tracy, CA, with end-to-end experience designing and deploying data visualizations using SQL, Tableau, Power BI, and SAP Business Objects — complete with filters, drill-downs, and parameters that let non-technical stakeholders actually use the data.

My path started in software QA, which still shapes how I work: every dashboard I ship is validated against the source data before it reaches an executive's screen. That combination — analytical build plus QA rigor — has taken me from testing web applications to briefing C-level leaders at Meta on the numbers that drive their decisions.

Beyond core BI tooling, I round out the data lifecycle with cloud data warehousing in Snowflake, CRM-driven insight through Salesforce Einstein, big-data platform work across Hive, Impala, Cloudera Manager, and HUE, DAX-based modeling in Microsoft Azure Databricks, and agile project tracking in Jira — giving me a full-stack view from raw data pipeline to boardroom-ready report.

I also bring practical AI fluency to the table — from prompt engineering to AI-assisted prototyping and analytics — which I use daily to work faster and surface insights sooner.

I hold Green Card EAD work authorization and I'm currently supporting the Executive (EXE) Support Engineering team at Meta.
